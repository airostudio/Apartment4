3. Rates & Pricing

All rates are quoted in Australian dollars (AUD) and are subject to change for future bookings. The rate applicable to your stay is the rate confirmed at the time of your booking.

Weekend rates (Friday–Sunday nights):

  • $855 per night
  • Minimum stay: 2 nights
  • No Saturday check-in is permitted. Weekend bookings must begin on a Friday.

Midweek rates (Monday–Thursday nights):

  • 2 or more nights: $855 per night
  • Single night: $985

Victorian School Holiday Premium: A surcharge of 30% applies to all nights that fall within Victorian Government-gazetted school holiday periods. This premium is automatically applied in the booking system when applicable dates are selected.

Cleaning fee: A flat fee of $150 per stay is charged in addition to the nightly rate. This covers standard end-of-stay cleaning. Additional cleaning charges may be applied if the property is left in an unreasonable condition (see Section 6).

Season note: Cascade Apartment 4 is a ski-season property. It is open June through August only. Bookings outside this window are not available. During the Green Season (September–May) the property is closed and not available for any bookings.

4. Check-in & Check-out

Check-in time: 4:00 PM on your arrival date.
Check-out time: 10:00 AM on your departure date.

Early check-in and late check-out cannot be guaranteed and are subject to availability. Please contact us in advance if you need to discuss your arrival time. Failure to vacate by 10:00 AM may result in an additional charge equivalent to one night's accommodation rate.

Saturday check-in is not permitted under any circumstances. Weekend stays must commence on a Friday.

Oversnow transport note: There is no vehicle access to Baw Baw Village during the ski season. All guests and their luggage must travel from Carpark 1 via oversnow transport (snowcat/snowmobile service operated by the resort). Guests are responsible for arranging and paying for this transport. Please allow adequate travel time before your scheduled check-in. We recommend contacting Mt Baw Baw Alpine Resort directly for current transport schedules and costs.

No vehicle access to the village: You cannot drive to the apartment door. Plan your arrival around the oversnow transport service from Carpark 1, and pack your luggage accordingly.

5. Occupancy

Cascade Apartment 4 accommodates a maximum of 7 guests at any one time. This includes adults, children and infants. Exceeding this occupancy limit is a breach of these Terms and may result in immediate termination of your stay without refund.

We understand that larger family or friend groups may wish to holiday together at Mt Baw Baw. We can coordinate group bookings of up to 21 guests across multiple properties. These arrangements must be made via direct enquiry — please contact us at hello@cascadeskiapartments.com or call +61 3 5165 1200 to discuss your requirements. Group bookings cannot be made through the standard online booking system.

6. Guest Responsibilities

By completing a booking you agree to treat the property and its contents with care and respect. Specific obligations include:

General care of the property: Guests must leave the property in a clean and tidy condition, consistent with the condition in which it was found on arrival. All rubbish must be disposed of appropriately. Furniture must not be moved without prior consent.

Linen & towels: Linen and towels are not provided. Guests must bring their own linen (fitted sheets, pillowcases, duvet covers) and towels, or arrange to hire these from a third-party linen hire service prior to arrival. Please plan accordingly.

Smoking: Smoking and vaping are strictly prohibited inside the property at all times. Smoking is only permitted outside in designated areas away from doors and windows. Evidence of smoking inside the property will result in a cleaning surcharge of at least $500.

Pets: No pets of any kind are permitted at the property, without exception.

Parties and events: The property is strictly for private residential use by the registered guests. Parties, events, functions or gatherings that include non-registered guests are not permitted. The maximum occupancy of 7 applies at all times, including during social visits.

Noise curfew: Out of respect for neighbours and the alpine community, guests must observe a noise curfew of 10:00 PM. After this time all noise must be kept to a level that cannot be heard outside the property. Failure to comply may result in removal from the property without refund.

Remember: Linen and towels are not supplied — you must bring your own or organise hire. This is one of the most common things guests forget, and there are no shops at the village to buy what you need.

7. Property Access & Alpine Conditions

Cascade Apartment 4 is located within Baw Baw Village at the summit of Mt Baw Baw. Access to the village during the ski season is subject to the policies and operations of Mt Baw Baw Alpine Resort.

  • No vehicle access: Private vehicles cannot be driven into the village. All guests must use the oversnow transport service that operates from Carpark 1 at the base of the mountain.
  • Road conditions: The road to Carpark 1 may be subject to closures, delays and compulsory chain requirements due to snow and ice. Check VicRoads and resort alerts before you travel.
  • WiFi: There is no resort-provided WiFi at Mt Baw Baw. Guests who require internet access during their stay should bring a mobile data device. 4G/5G coverage may be limited in the alpine environment.
  • Resort services: The availability of ski lifts, ski hire, food and beverage, and other resort amenities is at the discretion of Mt Baw Baw Alpine Resort and is not controlled by or guaranteed by Cascade Apartment 4.
