About the Apartment

A cosy alpine retreat tucked around the back of the Cascade complex for extra privacy

Cascade Apartment 4 is a self-contained, family-friendly unit tucked at the back of the Cascade complex at Baw Baw Village, Mt Baw Baw. It sleeps up to 7 across two bedrooms — a main bedroom with a queen bed and single bunk, and a second room with a tri-bunk. Settle into the cosy lounge with a gas log fireplace and DVD player, cook up a feast in the fully equipped kitchen, and use the private drying room and washing machine after a day in the snow. The apartment sits on the lower level with its own private entrance and dedicated snow play area — three toboggans are included for guest use.

Please note: Pillows and doonas are provided; guests must bring their own linen and towels (or arrange hire). During winter there is no vehicle access to the village — luggage drop is at Carpark 1. Snow chains are mandatory during snow season.
