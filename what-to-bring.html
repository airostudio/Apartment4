What to Bring

Everything you need for the perfect alpine stay at Mt Baw Baw

Linen & Towels — Hire Available

Bed linen (fitted sheets & pillowcases) and bath towels are not included in your booking. You can bring your own or hire linen sets at additional cost:

Queen/Double set — $40.00  ·  Single set — $20.00

Please contact us when booking to arrange linen hire. Bring your own towels and toiletries.

We provide: doonas, blankets & pillows for all beds

Already Provided — You Don't Need to Bring These

Doonas (all beds) Blankets Pillows Full kitchen (pots, pans, plates, cutlery) 2 Toboggans Tea & Coffee Toilet paper Hand soap Hairdryers Gas log fireplace DVD player Washing machine & dryer Ski boot drying room Dish soap & sponge Cooking oil spray Salt & pepper Laundry detergent
No WiFi within the resort — 4G mobile coverage is available. Consider downloading maps, music, and entertainment before you leave home.

Linen & Bedding

  • Queen/Double linen set — BYO or hire for $40
  • Single linen set — BYO or hire for $20
  • Pillowcases — included in linen hire sets above
  • Bath towels (1 per person) BYO
  • Hand towels & face washers BYO
  • Tea towels BYO
  • Doonas, blankets & pillows Provided ✓

Snow & Ski Gear

  • Skis / snowboard (hire available at resort) Optional
  • Ski boots (hire available at resort) Optional
  • Waterproof ski jacket & pants
  • Thermal underlayers (top & bottom)
  • Ski gloves or mittens
  • Beanie & neck gaiter
  • Ski goggles or sunglasses
  • Warm snow boots / après-ski footwear
  • 2 × toboggans Provided ✓ — extras available on request · missing toboggans charged at $120

Food & Groceries

  • Stock up before you head up — limited supplies at the resort shop
  • Breakfast foods & snacks for the mountain
  • Dinners & lunches for every day of your stay
  • Drinks, wine, beer & soft drinks
  • Cooking oil spray Provided ✓
  • Salt & pepper Provided ✓
  • Other condiments & spices BYO
  • Dish soap & sponge Provided ✓
  • Laundry detergent Provided ✓

Personal Essentials

  • Tea & coffee Provided ✓
  • Toilet paper Provided ✓
  • Hand soap Provided ✓
  • Hairdryers Provided ✓
  • Toiletries (shampoo, conditioner, body wash) BYO
  • Sunscreen (SPF 50+ — alpine UV is intense)
  • Lip balm with SPF
  • Any prescription medications
  • First aid kit (basic)
  • Warm indoor clothing & slippers
  • Portable phone charger / power bank

Getting There

  • Easy access to the apartment from Carpark 1 — park at the base and you're just a short walk away
  • Snow chains — MANDATORY during ski season. You must carry them at all times on the mountain road. Where to hire chains →
  • 4WD or AWD vehicle recommended
  • Easy-carry bags & backpacks for the walk from Carpark 1
  • Mt Baw Baw Resort entry pass / lift tickets

Entertainment & Kids

  • DVDs (DVD player provided)
  • Books, magazines & puzzles
  • Board games & card games
  • Offline games & entertainment on tablets
  • Baby/toddler items (cot, high chair) — not provided, BYO if needed
  • Portable speaker (Bluetooth)

Packing tip: The apartment is easy to reach on foot from Carpark 1. Pack heavier items like groceries in separate bags from clothing and gear so everything stays manageable on the walk up.

Please Do Not Bring

  • Pets — no animals permitted at the property
  • Smoking — strictly no smoking inside the apartment
  • Party supplies — the apartment is a family-friendly property; large gatherings are not permitted
  • Additional guests beyond the 7-person maximum (unless a group booking has been arranged)
